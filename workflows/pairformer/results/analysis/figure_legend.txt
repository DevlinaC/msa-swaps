MSA-Pairformer contact-map similarity for 362 swapped MSAs. Predictions used up to 512 sequences per MSA with query weighting disabled (uniform sequence weights). The query sequence was retained, and deeper alignments underwent deterministic diversity selection without HHfilter. Each swapped map was compared with its proper query (red) and donor (gold) reference maps at matched residue positions. Panels show (A) ordinary top-L Jaccard, (B) rank-tolerant top-L Jaccard, (C) Pearson correlation over all eligible residue pairs, and (D) Pearson correlation over the union of the two top-L sets, at minimum sequence separations of 6, 12, and 24 residues. L is the length of the common registered overlap; rank tolerance credits a top-L pair found within the other map’s top floor(1.5L) pairs, using the strict top-L union as denominator. Cutoff ties are retained. Each point represents one swap (362 per group); jitter is horizontal only, and black bars mark medians.
