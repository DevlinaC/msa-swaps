Figure S5. MSA-derived contacts are rarely realized in AlphaFold structures. The fraction of residue–residue contacts inferred from each donor-swapped MSA that are present in AlphaFold structures predicted using the swapped MSA, the query sequence alone, or the query with its native full MSA. Contacts were inferred independently using (A) GREMLIN and (B) MSA Transformer. For GREMLIN, contacts were restricted to residue pairs with z-scores ≥1 and sequence separation (|i-j| ≥ 5). For MSA Transformer, the top L predicted contacts with (|i-j| ≥ 6) were analyzed, where L is the query length. Structural contacts were defined by Cβ–Cβ distances ≤8 Å (Cα for Gly). When the inferred contact map exceeded the length of the predicted structure, contact overlap was maximized over global residue-index offsets from −5 to +5 positions. Swapped distributions contain 9,050 predictions (362 query–donor MSA combinations × 25 structures); others contain 850 (34 targets × 25 structures). Median fractions of inferred contacts realized in the AF2 swapped-MSA, single-sequence, and full-MSA predictions were 3.21%, 3.76%, and 5.51%, respectively, for GREMLIN and 3.39%, 3.43%, and 4.98% for MSA Transformer. Corresponding medians for AF3 were 5.06%, 4.70%, and 5.52% for GREMLIN and 4.00%, 4.44%, and 5.00% for MSA Transformer. Horizontal lines indicate medians. Thus, structures predicted using donor-swapped MSAs do not preferentially realize the MSA-derived contacts relative to structures predicted from the query sequence alone or with its native MSA.  The few most notable rare exceptions were 1dtk swapped with 2jvv, 1dtk swapped with 1cqu, and 2k9h swapped with 1cqu, which have more donor-like contacts than other swaps but do not assume donor-liked structures.
