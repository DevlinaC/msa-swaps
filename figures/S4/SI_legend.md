Figure S4. Agreement between MSA-derived contacts with native and swapped-input predictions. A. Contact precision. B. Jaccard overlap. Both panels show AF2 and AF3 predictions evaluated with GREMLIN and MSA Transformer at minimum sequence separations |i−j| ≥6, ≥12, and ≥24. Distributions compare native MSA → native fold (light gray), swapped MSA → native query (red), swapped MSA → native donor (gold), and swapped MSA → swapped output (dark gray). Native baselines include 34 proteins; each swapped distribution includes 362 comparisons. Boxes show the interquartile range, central lines indicate medians, whiskers extend to observations within 1.5 interquartile ranges, and points indicate observations beyond the whiskers.
