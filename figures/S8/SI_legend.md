Figure S8. Comparison of AF3 structures with MSA-derived contact predictions for BCCIP and RfaH/NusG. A. BCCIPα and BCCIPβ. B. RfaH and NusG. Representative AF3 structures are shown alongside MSA Pairformer contact predictions and structure-derived contact maps. Pearson correlations and contact-set overlaps quantify similarity between the query–MSA combinations and agreement with the resulting structures.  For the BCCIP isoforms, the whole structures are used for the calculations; for RfaH/NusG, only contacts involving the C-terminal domain (CTD) are used.
