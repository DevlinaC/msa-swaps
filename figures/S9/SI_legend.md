Figure S9. The RfaH CTD adopts a β-sheet fold in the NusG–RfaH chimera. A. Schematic of the 183-residue chimera. The analyzed RfaH CTD (wild-type residues 110–162) maps identically to residues 131–183 of the chimera. B. Highest-ranked AF2 (ColabFold) and AF3 predictions, with the CTD in red and the remaining residues in gray. Mean pLDDT is shown for the complete model and for the CTD separately. C. CTD structural states and confidence across all 25 predictions per method. All 50 predictions contain a β-sheet CTD; none adopts the α-helical RfaH hairpin. Points show individual mean CTD pLDDT values; black lines indicate medians, and the dashed line marks pLDDT 70. CTD mean pLDDT is ≥70 for 18/25 AF2 predictions and 25/25 AF3 predictions. Each method used its own sequence-derived MSA without templates; structural-state counts include all predictions regardless of confidence.  D.  Superimposed 1H-15N HSQCs of the beta-sheet RfaH CTD (black) and the NusG-RfaH chimera (blue).  Most black peaks overlap with blue, indicating the RfaH CTD assumes the beta-roll fold in the chimera.  E.  Characteristic peaks of autoinhibited helical RfaH CTD are not observed in the NusG-RfaH chimera.
